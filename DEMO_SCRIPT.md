# Staker Space Vault - Demo Video Script

**Duration:** 3-5 minutes
**Recording tool:** Loom, OBS, or screen recording

---

## Intro (30 sec)

**[Show landing page]**

"Hey! I'm showcasing Staker Space Vault - an autonomous staking vault for Solana.

The problem: Staking on Solana means choosing validators. Most people either pick the biggest ones - hurting decentralization - or don't know how to evaluate quality.

Our solution: Deposit SOL, let an AI agent stake to quality decentralized validators automatically."

---

## How It Works (45 sec)

**[Scroll to "How It Works" section]**

"Here's how it works:

1. You deposit SOL to a shared vault
2. Our agent analyzes validators using StakeWiz data
3. It stakes to validators matching strict criteria:
   - Under 1 million SOL stake - supporting decentralization
   - Low commission - 5% or less
   - Low MEV fees - 10% or less
   - High uptime - over 95%

4. You earn staking + MEV rewards
5. Unstake anytime with a ~2 day cooldown"

## Recording Tips

1. Use 1080p or higher
2. Clear browser tabs
3. Have devnet SOL ready
4. Practice the flow once before recording
5. Speak clearly, not too fast
6. Show the Agent Activity panel prominently - that's the "agent" differentiator!
